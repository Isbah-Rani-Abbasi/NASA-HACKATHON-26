PROMPT 1: Build the complete game
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Act as a Senior Frontend Game Developer, Motion UI Designer, and Space Simulation Architect. Build a complete, kid-friendly (ages 7–12+), interactive space-themed web game for the 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge titled **"CHRONO-OUTPOST: Rewrite the Mission"**.

### 1. TECH STACK & PROJECT STRUCTURE (BACKEND-READY)
- **Framework:** React (Vite) + Tailwind CSS v4 (`@import "tailwindcss";` in `src/index.css` with `@tailwindcss/vite` in `vite.config.js`).
- **Installed Libraries:** `react-router-dom`, `axios`, `motion` (`import { motion, AnimatePresence } from "motion/react"`), `lucide-react`, `canvas-confetti`.
- **Backend-Ready Service Layer (`src/services/apiClient.js`):**
  - Configure an Axios instance with `baseURL: import.meta.env.VITE_API_BASE_URL || ""` so the frontend can seamlessly plug into a Node.js/Express backend later without refactoring components.
  - Create `src/services/spaceWeatherService.js` that fetches live space weather events (`FLR`, `CME`, `SEP`) from NASA's CCMC DONKI API (`https://ccmc.gsfc.nasa.gov/DONKI-API/get/`) and automatically falls back to a bundled `src/data/donki_fallback.json` file if offline or rate-limited.
- **Audio Engine (`src/utils/soundEffects.js`):** Use the browser's built-in Web Audio API (no external MP3 files needed) to synthesize sci-fi button clicks, mascot voice beeps, meteoroid whooshes, alarm sirens, engine explosion rumbles, and victory fanfare (with a global Mute/Unmute toggle in the top bar).

### 2. VISUAL THEME & GLOBAL ANIMATIONS
- **Color Palette:** Deep Cosmic Navy (`#050816`), Nebula Indigo (`#0F172A`), Glowing Cyan (`#00F0FF`), Solar Amber (`#FFB800`), Mars Crimson (`#FF3B5C`), and Bio-Green (`#00E676`).
- **Persistent Space Canvas (`src/components/SpaceBackdrop.jsx`):** A smooth 60fps HTML5 `<canvas>` rendering multi-layered twinkling stars, drifting nebula clouds, slowly rotating planets, and occasional shooting meteoroids with glowing tails.
- **Interactive Mascot (`src/components/AstroBotMascot.jsx`):** A cute, floating animated robot astronaut SVG ("ASTRO-BOT") with blinking LED eyes, hovering thruster flame, speech bubbles with typewriter text effect, **"Next →"** and **"Skip"** buttons, and a **Spotlight Guide Mode** that highlights specific UI elements during tutorials.

### 3. COMPLETE USER FLOW & PAGES (STRICT SEQUENCE)

#### PAGE 1: Landing Page (`/`)
- **Visuals:** Full-screen animated deep-space canvas with rotating Earth, Moon, and Mars in the background and shooting meteoroids streaking across the frame.
- **Mascot Onboarding Sequence:** Astro-Bot flies into the center-left of the screen and guides the young player step-by-step through 3 short, engaging dialogue steps:
  1. *"Welcome, Junior Commander! Famous NASA missions like Apollo 13 and Mars Polar Lander ran into sudden space storms and disasters."*
  2. *"Inside the Chrono-Simulator, you will pilot through the storm, survive the crash landing, and manage a real space outpost!"*
  3. *"Balance Oxygen, Shields, Power, Food, and Science to turn famous NASA failures into historic victories!"*
- **Action:** After the mascot finishes (or if the user clicks "Skip Intro"), reveal a large, pulsing glowing CTA button: **"MOVE TO COMPETITION →"** navigating to `/sectors`.

#### PAGE 2: Sector Selection Hub (`/sectors`)
- **Visuals:** Same cohesive cosmic color palette as the landing page, featuring a different animated space scene (orbiting satellites, solar wind waves, and a central Sun radar).
- **Layout:** Two large, interactive **Square Mission Sector Cards** side-by-side with hover scale/glow effects:
  1. **Square Card 1: `MISSION ON MOON`** — Features a rotating, cratered 3D-shaded Moon illustration, gravity/atmosphere stats (`16.6% Gravity | No Atmosphere`), and **"ENTER LUNAR SECTOR"** button.
  2. **Square Card 2: `MISSION ON MARS`** — Features a rotating red Martian globe with dust swirls, stats (`38% Gravity | Thin CO2 Air | Comms Delay`), and **"ENTER MARTIAN SECTOR"** button.

#### PAGE 3A: Moon Mission Briefing (`/moon`)
- **Visuals:** Full Moon-themed animated environment—panoramic lunar horizon at the bottom, rising Earth in the black sky, floating lunar dust particles, and twinkling stars.
- **Competition List:** Displays available Lunar Sector challenges:
  - **Active Mission Card:** **APOLLO 13 — "The Lunar Lifeboat" (1970)** (Explain that an oxygen tank rupture crippled the ship; player must survive the meteoroid storm and run the emergency lunar outpost). Clicking **"LAUNCH MISSION"** navigates to `/cockpit/apollo-13`.
  - Include 1–2 locked preview cards (*"Artemis Base Alpha — Coming Soon"*) so it feels like a real competition hub.

#### PAGE 3B: Mars Mission Briefing (`/mars`)
- **Visuals:** Full Mars-themed animated environment—red rocky canyons, blowing Martian dust storms across the screen, and two tiny moons (Phobos & Deimos) orbiting above.
- **Competition List:** Displays Martian Sector challenges:
  - **Active Mission Card:** **MARS POLAR LANDER — "The Red Storm" (1999)** (Explain that solar flares and descent anomalies threatened the lander; player must dodge incoming solar flares and stabilize the Martian outpost). Clicking **"LAUNCH MISSION"** navigates to `/cockpit/mars-lander`.
  - Include 1–2 locked preview cards (*"Olympus Mons Outpost — Coming Soon"*).

#### PAGE 4: Cockpit POV Action Sequence (`/cockpit/:missionId`)
- **Visuals (First-Person Cockpit POV):** Render a metallic spaceship cockpit frame (windshield struts, glowing dashboard instruments, radar screen) looking out into space toward either the Moon or Mars.
- **15-Second Interactive Dodge Mini-Game:**
  - **Controls:** Player moves the spacecraft left/right/up/down using **Mouse/Touch drag** or **Arrow/WASD keys** to dodge incoming hazards.
  - **Moon Variant:** Glowing flaming **Meteoroids** hurtling toward the windshield.
  - **Mars Variant:** Intense **NASA DONKI Solar Flare Plasma Bolts** streaking across space.
  - **Performance Link:** Track `hullIntegrity` (starts at 100%). Dodging hazards preserves starting power for the next stage!
- **Scripted Engine Explosion Finale:** At the 15-second mark, a critical red warning flashes (`"WARNING: MAIN ENGINE OVERLOAD!"`), an unavoidable shockwave hits, the cockpit shakes (`CSS shake animation`), sparks/fire particles explode across the windshield (`HTML5 Canvas explosion`), and the screen transitions smoothly to the Outpost Survival page.

#### PAGE 5: Final Outpost Survival Game (`/outpost/:missionId`)
- **Environment Theme:**
  - If `apollo-13`: Animated **Lunar Surface Outpost** (gray craters, solar panels, protective dome, Earth in the sky).
  - If `mars-lander`: Animated **Martian Surface Outpost** (red dunes, blowing dust particles, solar arrays, pinkish-amber sky).
- **Mascot Interactive Tutorial (Spotlight Guide):**
  - Before Turn 1 starts, Astro-Bot appears and highlights each of the **5 Outpost Subsystems in strict increasing priority order (Tier 5 Highest $\rightarrow$ Tier 1 Lowest)** with a glowing spotlight ring around each slider:
    1. **Tier 5 (CRITICAL): Life Support ($O_2$ & Water)** — *"Never let this hit 0%, or the mission fails!"*
    2. **Tier 4 (HIGH): Radiation Shielding** — *"Boost this when NASA DONKI detects a Solar Flare!"*
    3. **Tier 3 (MEDIUM-HIGH): Power Generation** — *"Produces the energy points you distribute each day."*
    4. **Tier 2 (MEDIUM): Food Production** — *"Feeds your crew; you may need to dim grow-lights during storms."*
    5. **Tier 1 (WIN GOAL): Science Ops** — *"Lowest survival priority, but reach 100% Science to rewrite history and win!"*
- **Core Gameplay Loop (6 Turns / Sols):**
  - Each turn displays a real **NASA DONKI Space Weather Alert** (`FLR` flare class, `CME` speed, or `SEP` particle storm) translated into kid-friendly instructions.
  - Player adjusts power sliders across the 5 priority tiers within their `Available Power` budget and clicks **"EXECUTE SOL"**.
  - Play a 2-second visual animation on the outpost (shield dome glowing against solar rays, solar panels charging, greenhouses lighting up) and update all 5 subsystem bars.
- **Endings:**
  - **Victory (`Science Ops === 100%` and `Life Support > 0%`):** Trigger `canvas-confetti`, unlock a collectible **"History Rewritten Mission Patch"**, and show a side-by-side comparison of **Real NASA History vs. How You Saved the Mission**.
  - **Failure (`Life Support <= 0%` or `Crew Health <= 0%`):** Show which subsystem failed with an encouraging tip from Astro-Bot and a **"RETRY MISSION"** button.
